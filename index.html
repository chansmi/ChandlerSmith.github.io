Chandler Smith

DPhil student, University of Oxford · Student Researcher, Google DeepMind

I am a DPhil student in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, advised by Philip Torr and Christian Schroeder de Witt in the Torr Vision Group, and a Cooperative AI PhD Scholar. I am currently a student researcher at Google DeepMind on the Discovery team. I am also a research analyst at the Cooperative AI Foundation and a research affiliate at the Oxford Martin AI Governance Institute.

As AI systems become more autonomous and interconnected, they introduce risks that extend beyond the safety of any individual system. My research focuses on understanding, measuring, and mitigating these risks: multi-agent security, cooperative AI, AI control, and the evaluation of agentic systems.

Before Oxford, I was a research scholar at MATS, a technical architect fellow at In-Q-Tel working on AI and national security, and an engineer at Dimagi working on global health. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Northeastern University.
